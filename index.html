About Me

Hi! I'm Nate Brewer, an Applied Software Engineering student at Northern Kentucky University (graduating May 2026) with a passion for building systems that push boundaries - from distributed edge computing networks and IoT hardware to full-stack web applications and AI-integrated pipelines.

My work spans multiple layers of the stack: I've architected multi-threaded Java distributed systems, written low-level C++ firmware for ESP32/LoRa hardware, built cross-platform apps in Flutter/Dart, and integrated production-grade AI models like SepFormer and Wav2Vec2 into real pipelines.

I'm also a published research co-author, with papers accepted to IEEE AIIoT 2026 and EIT 2026. I approach development with discipline - I spec requirements before writing a single line, design with extensibility in mind, and treat AI tools as collaborative executors rather than shortcuts. The result is clean, well-planned code that actually holds up. When I'm not building, I'm researching. Whether it's semantic voice compression for LoRa mesh networks or spatial audio sensing for disaster response environments, I gravitate toward problems that are technically hard and genuinely meaningful.
